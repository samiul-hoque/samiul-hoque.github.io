Presented at IECON 2026 in Doha, Qatar, 18–21 October 2026 (paper
IECON26-000573). The IEEE Xplore record and DOI will be added once the
proceedings are published.

The platform paper from the thesis work: a four-wheel mecanum robot on a
single ESP32, with no single-board computer and no ROS, at a USD 173 bill
of materials. Its main feature is an on-robot routine that equalises
per-motor velocity using only the robot's own encoders, cutting the
inter-wheel velocity spread by roughly a factor of six. Hardware,
firmware and software are open source at
[samiul-hoque/open-omnibot](https://github.com/samiul-hoque/open-omnibot).
